# Bird & Vine Plates — bird library sources

All 43 birds are public-domain watercolours from the Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam (Public Domain Mark 1.0).
Artists: Robert Jacob Gordon (Cape Colony, 1777–86), Anselmus Boëtius de Boodt (bird albums for Rudolf II, 1596–1610), Jan Brandes (1785–87) and Johannes Bronkhorst (c. 1675–1726).
Only birds whose feet and claws are drawn clear of any branch or rock are included, so each can stand on the generated vine.
Processing: background removed with rembg (isnet-general-use), white point lifted, resized, saved as WebP; claws restored from the original scan where noted in library.json. Two de Boodt album pages (oriole, blackbird) are drawn sideways and were rotated 90°.
