# Bird & Vine Plates — bird library sources

All 52 birds are public-domain watercolours from the Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam (Public Domain Mark 1.0).
Artists: Robert Jacob Gordon (Cape Colony, 1777–86), Anselmus Boëtius de Boodt (bird albums for Rudolf II, 1596–1610), Jan Brandes (1785–87) and Johannes Bronkhorst (c. 1675–1726).
Processing: background removed with rembg (isnet-general-use), painted perches masked where needed, white point lifted, resized, saved as WebP. Two de Boodt album pages (oriole, blackbird) are drawn sideways on the page and were rotated 90°.
